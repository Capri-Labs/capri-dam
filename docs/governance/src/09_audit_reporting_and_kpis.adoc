== Audit, Reporting & KPIs

=== What's captured automatically

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Source | Captures

| `audit_logs`
| Every create/update/destroy on an auditable model (assets, folders,
  metadata schemas, workflows, users, groups, policies, etc.), plus
  administrative actions like impersonation grants and password resets.
  Immutable — protected by a database trigger.

| Workflow `audit_log` (per instance)
| The step-by-step history of a single workflow instance (started,
  approved/rejected per step, force-cancelled, reassigned).

| Data Health dashboard
| Aggregate connector health, ingestion error rates, and quarantine review
  backlog — operational signal for ingestion governance.

| Duplicate Manager stats
| Counts of pending/resolved/dismissed duplicate groups — signal for content
  hygiene governance.

| AI model config health checks
| `health_status`/`last_health_check_at`/`health_latency_ms` per AI provider
  — operational signal for vendor governance.
|===

=== Recommended governance KPIs

These are not built-in platform metrics (with the exception of the
dashboards noted above) — they're recommended for a governance owner to track
periodically, using the audit log and admin screens as the source data:

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| KPI | Why it matters

| # of folder/collection policies with `explicit_deny` set
| A growing number suggests the group hierarchy itself may need
  restructuring rather than continuing to patch with exceptions.

| Access-review completion rate (per quarter)
| Tracks whether folder/collection policy group membership is actually being
  re-certified against current team structure, not just set once and
  forgotten.

| % of assets under an applied metadata schema vs. schema-less
| Low coverage indicates a taxonomy-governance gap — assets without a schema
  are harder to search, export, and govern consistently.

| Quarantined-asset review backlog / average time-to-review
| A growing backlog is an early signal of an ingestion connector or
  taxonomy problem that needs a change (see
  <<section-change-and-release-management,Change & Release Management>>).

| Duplicate-group resolution backlog
| Unresolved duplicate groups are unmanaged storage cost and a source-of-
  truth risk (see the LinkedIn post that inspired this doc set — "which
  `hero_final_really_final.jpg` is the real one?").

| Recycle Bin items approaching auto-purge without review
| Confirms the retention window is being actively used as a safety net, not
  bypassed by immediate hard-deletes elsewhere.

| Impersonation-action count (per period)
| Any use should be attributable and, ideally, rare — a spike warrants a
  quick review of why.
|===

=== Recommended reporting cadence

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Cadence | Review

| Weekly (operational)
| Quarantine backlog, duplicate-group backlog, ingestion connector health.

| Quarterly (governance)
| Folder/collection access re-certification, metadata-schema coverage,
  `explicit_deny` usage review.

| Annually (compliance)
| Full compliance-mapping review against
  <<section-compliance-and-regulatory-mapping,Compliance & Regulatory
  Mapping>>; vendor risk re-assessment for all active storage/CDN/AI/
  ingestion vendors.
|===
